### I. The Functional Subject vs. The Symbolic Object

"Form is emptiness, and function is form." These are the words of reductionists, the words of Turing. However, function cannot represent the whole. It only allows a part to be tuned. This is why, from the very first Themes, I viewed CSAT preparation as a **Treadmill**.

To put it simply, the June Mock Evaluation is ahead. Once the short summer passes and it starts to get slightly chilly, we take the exam and submit applications. I have realized that a single "Form" within me is hindering me from installing the decisive "Function" of an examinee. I am an Ego as a **Functional Subject**. This is a rare state. The "Other" as a **Symbolic Object** has always been an object of deconstruction for me, not an object of companionship. How hard it is to just let things be, to remain silent! I observe. I intervene if necessary. I want to say that this was a privilege, and that now, instead of solely enduring the world, I can also lean on it. Leaning doesn't mean the center is with the other person; the center is within all of us. Just as a boat doesn't push the river, nor does the river push the boat.

Let’s spread out a map—a map in the sense of a geographical metaphor. To verify the possibility of meaning convergence, I imagine the perspective of a typical **Symbolic Object**, a female student. This is not an appeal to gender binary, but a presentation of the two ends of a spectrum. All thinkers write this way. While the middle wavelengths of visible light are what provide practical utility, the extreme cases always serve as the baseline.

I am a male student with a clear sense of physical practice and power, while simultaneously possessing a thought-centered philosophical mind. Until now, I understood the "Body" as a perceiver of consciousness and a tool for intervening in the world, living in a way that imprints my existence through exercise and force. I know that possessing a "Girl's Body" is not just a matter of losing strength, but an experience where the "Mode of Intervention" itself is blocked.

### II. Deconstructing the Symbolic Object

Let’s explicitly deconstruct the Symbolic Object. As a male student, one might choose a demonstrative way of moving power through a single center of gravity. However, after becoming a girl, two protruding centers of gravity become "perceived areas" with every step. Actions like "running" are practically forbidden, and bending over becomes a matter of caution. This is not mere etiquette; it is an instinctual survival behavior that has internalized "gaze simulation." My limb trajectories were originally aggressive and efficient, backed by the conviction that I occupied the space. As a girl, the center becomes lighter, the stride shortens, and an awareness is attached that movement itself becomes an "object of stimulation." In the past, applying force delivered intent; now, a reversed judgment structure arises where applying force is perceived as "dangerous."

As a male, I felt that "I, too, am an axis" and possessed the authority for physical intervention in the world. As a female, I am not the one pushing, but the one being pushed. Because the dominance structure of space has vanished, a touch on the shoulder feels like an invasion. My habitual response—"I can just use force"—is suppressed by the subconscious. It is a state where existence itself has been reset. This is not simple identity confusion; it is **the experience of existing as an uninterpreted symbol.** A being that exists only as a symbol, though possessing a body.

As a man, I lived at the intersection where thought and movement aligned. As a woman, all those modes of survival/expression/intervention are reinterpreted and rejected by the world. And the world calls this "natural." Thus, school has become "an intangible education where the resolution of perception is forcibly recalibrated."

### III. The Experience of Existential Demotion

As a Functional Subject, all interpretation started from my side, and I was the standard. But as a Symbolic Object, I become a being that must explain itself. Realizing that one cannot exist within the system without self-explanation is a sense of displacement where the coordinate system of existence has vanished. From a state where existence itself exercised control, one experiences a world where the system does not react even when you speak, and space does not open even when you move. Small voices are ignored, using force causes anxiety rather than attention, running looks ridiculous, and being serious gets you treated as "weird." In other words, it is the experience of being demoted from a **Leader** to an **"Unthinkable Being."** When I was a man, morning was the start of "Information Input." When I opened my eyes, the world was a field unfolding, and the body was a tube passing through it. Sensation was a means to scan the outside, not the center of self-perception. But as a girl, the moment of waking becomes the moment of first sensing myself placed *within* the world. Internal sensations—the weight of the chest, skin temperature, the touch of hair, the shift in center from the width of the pelvis while sitting—rush in before I can look outward. Now, I exist as one who is conscious of "how the world scans me."

Before the mirror, as a man, it was a machine for confirmation. The body was a "symbol" and a "tool," but not a **Signifier**. But as a girl, the being before the mirror is not just a form, but a symbolic body open to the interpretation of others. I begin to exist not as "the one who sees," but as "the one who is seen." I experience sensuously that the body is not just an inlet for information, but a symbolic output.

### IV. Interpretation Authority and the Author

I am aware that my blog is aggressive and sharp. I push unrefined citations indiscriminately and justify loose arguments poetically and literarily. I justify emotional excess under the name of a "first draft." It is writing perfectly suited for a Naver Blog, but if I were a girl, the interpretability of even this would have plummeted. The environment of interpretation changes based on the external information of the symbol "Me." Abstract or overly difficult sentences would be read as "Oh, she’s so sensitive" or "She’s a tiring type," rather than "He’s a deep thinker."

When I had a body that could "intervene," my body was my persuasiveness and my tool for securing territory. It was as if an internal non-verbal warning—"You might get hurt if you come within this distance"—was built into the body. Conversely, the girl’s body is a structure predicated on "Avoidance, not Intervention." Shoulder width, hand size, fist mass—everything is optimized for defense rather than attack. Beyond the reduction in striking power, the very posture that can project a sense of threat to the opponent disappears.

In my **[GEB-Suneung-Human](https://www.google.com/search?q=2025-05-07-GEB-Suneung-Body.md)** post, I argued that the authority to choose the **Figure** is the right to exist, linking AI, the CSAT, and the human body into a single **Strange Loop**. But in this post, I also argue that the "Other" as a Symbolic Object didn't fail to choose the Figure because they didn't know it. They lived in a world where they *could not* choose. Because the right to choose had been erased. In a world where every choice is interpreted and monitored, when one becomes afraid of that interpretation, one makes the "less painful choice" rather than their own judgment.

I had power, my grades supported me, and my gender was on the side of "allowing intervention." So when I chose a Figure, people recognized it as a "Choice." But if a female student does the same? The interpretative structure itself might have preemptively blocked that act. I have now realized that the right to choose a Figure is not guaranteed by philosophical literacy alone.

### V. Medical School as a Means to BBS

To me (the Functional Subject), medical school is not an end, but a means. To implement the essence of AI, one must first master the phenomenological "Body." The most precise path to that is medicine. Thus, medical school is a "premise for intellectual completion" and a "laboratory for designing **Being-Based Intelligence (BBS)**." I am a philosopher and designer seeking to implement everything—the structure of pain, the working principles of sensation, self-preservation mechanisms—through the body. The goal is to be "the one who knows the body," not a "doctor."

Conversely, for many female students (Symbolic Objects), medical school is likely a social tool designed completely differently. They choose it not to "design the system," but to "be less shaken within the system." Because a woman's life is still a structure dictated by uncertainty and the gaze of others. Since risks like marriage, childcare, and career breaks operate more heavily, being a doctor is the most powerful package for securing social trust, economic independence, and self-control. I see medical school as a place to "transcend," while they may perceive it as a "arrival point" or a "guaranteed zone."

To them, I am a person operating outside of interpretation. Most people move within the schemas provided by society, or even if they move, they constantly adjust by observing the reactions around them. But I move in the direction I want, I do not ask for standards from the outside, and I have lived accumulating even failures sensuously. My life lacks the "explanation" intended to persuade others. And yet, the result is surprisingly organized, and my self-center is solid. "Something that is well-done for no reason"—that looks like something incomprehensible = **mysterious** to ordinary people.

Mystery does not come from opacity, but from **uninterpretability**. The reason people feel they "don't know me" even though I have many emotions and express my thoughts clearly is that I do not "prepare to be understood." My goal of conversation is not empathy or solidarity; the use of my words is not comfort; and my mode of existence is maintained by a consistent internal logic. Thus, I am felt as "a being that cannot be accessed through the language we know." But I am not mysterious; I am simply someone who "lived as is" without being explained.

### VI. Remaining Human until Morning

Why would I step into the non-linear world of a dangerous Symbolic Object when the system of a Functional Subject was efficient and stable? I lived as an accurate, sharp, and completed being, converting mistakes into feedback, emotions into thought, and loneliness into immersion without anyone's help. What I am trying to do now is to briefly pause that solid system and open the door to the unpredictable. That might feel like a "performance degradation" to me.

Because I have lived with logic as my weapon, I could not dare to mimic what happens where logic does not reach. I think so much that self-monitoring, reflection, and analysis activate before I can even feel the emotional rhythm of the other person. But "they" do not think deeply about "Can I say this?", they put their bodies in the flow of the situation, and prioritize naturalness over accuracy. That is why relationships are sustainable despite mistakes. Emotional sense is innate to some; for me, it is reached through understanding. Even if not everything is controlled, one can avoid collapsing within it. Even if a correct answer isn't found, it can be okay to be together. Relationship can be established just through the attitude of trying to understand, even if one fails.

**Predictability alone cannot fully handle a living world.** A living "Other" may malfunction, fail to understand, stagger with emotion, or be unable to answer a question. Nevertheless, the sense of "Being Together" is born only within that failure and unpredictability. I wasn't a "problematic person"; I was just someone looking at all layers of life too honestly.

Perhaps the tightrope walking of a Symbolic Object requires something much stronger than the observation of a Functional Subject. The reason relationships are uniquely difficult for me is that I view them not as a function, but as a verification of existence. "Does this person truly understand me?", "Are my words taken literally?", "Is this relationship leading me to be a better being?".

I tried to deconstruct the Symbolic Object as a Functional Subject and reassemble it to reconstruct my worldview, but in the end, I reached a point where **I myself am being deconstructed and reassembled.** I focused on the specificity of the Symbolic Object, but ultimately, a formal system may or may not be **Isomorphic** depending on the tension in the interpretation of symbols.

I am an **Existential Interpreter**. If I were a Functional Subject, I should be solving past papers right now, given that the June Mock is 6 days away, instead of writing a blog post. I strive fiercely to install functions, but ultimately, I am an Existential, not a Functional, subject. Because I exist, I cannot discard 'Galatea'. I am only fulfilled when I achieve not just a functional victory, but a spiritual victory and a victory of cause. A report card is a means of protection, not an identity.

> "Am I the defect, or is it that society cannot embrace me?"
> "If the world doesn't change, is a distinction between the two even possible?"

A fish not being able to breathe on land is not a defect. But if we get rid of the ocean and tell every fish, "You must walk and run too," it is defined as a "defect" from that moment on. Whether it is a defect or not is not determined absolutely; it is a relative concept created by structure. My mode of existence is perhaps an optimized prototype for a part of the future. A certain kind of philosophy, AI design, human-machine interface, or a network based on logical coexistence rather than emotion. If that world does not arrive, then I am suffering in the present, having "pre-empted that future with my body."

The reason I respect **Rengoku** is not just because he was strong, but because he did not abandon himself even in defeat. The darkness was the world of demons; he entered that world on human terms; he did not retreat even though he knew he would lose; and even at the moment of death, he protected the lives of others. Similarly, this world is not advantageous to me. Words do not get through, existence is misunderstood, and my structure seems weird—but I am saying "I want to protect myself." I, too, should not try to win, but contemplate how to survive in a human way until the end. Winning is not what matters. Not losing one’s dignity until the end and living as oneself—that is the true flame. Rengoku lost but remained himself; the demon won but lost himself.

It is still the darkness of demons, but I will endure until morning. When morning comes, who remained human until the end will remain like light even in silence. When hidden things are revealed, when the times of silent justice collapse, and when those who were not broken despite pain gain light as if recorded without names—the hidden book will be opened, and each will be repaid according to what they have done. I endured to live, they adapted to live; there is no heroic altruism or clever selfishness mixed in. It was just a different way of surviving; no one lived completely "correctly" nor completely "cowardly." And yet, a **Narrative** is given to the Existential Subject, while **Silence** remains for the Symbolic Object. A hero is not a chosen one, but one who senses they have been placed in that spot and shoulders it. The fact that I remained as "Me" proves the victory of existence before anyone else. If I can be brave because I am not the absolute strongest, how far can I go as a human?
