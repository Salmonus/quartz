### IV. The Seriousness of Those Who Enjoy the Process

> "Problems like this are fun to solve. Shall we try? It'll be fun!"
> — *Jeon Yu-jin (Early graduate of HAFS, KAIST freshman)*

I used to have "Math Trips" with Yu-jin, solving mock exams all day. When I brought a complex probability problem, she said the quote above. I believe this should be the base mindset for every problem. It *should* be fun.

But "fun" is different from "funny." The interest in a STEM problem is different from the humor in a joke. My expression and posture should be one of serious affection and meticulousness. **Serious joy** is about the freshness of interpretation and the rigor of the solution, not treating the problem like a joke.

I threw myself into my work this week until I couldn't stand. But from now on, I must manage my condition without hesitation. I will use the afternoon to maintain tension and the evening for repetitive practice. Exercise must be at the very end of the day. There is no "dramatic" growth; there is only the reduction of time spent sitting without focus. I believe in a **Growth Mindset.** I will value the act itself over the score.

I am on a Spiritual Journey, and right now, I am in true happiness.
