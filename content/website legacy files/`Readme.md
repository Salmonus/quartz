#### **Title: The Architecture of Survival: Intellectual Traces of a 19-Year-Old Mind**

**Context & Background**
This collection, preserved in the 'legacy files' folder, is a raw and unedited archive of a transformative period in the life of Kim Yeon-woo. Written during the final year of high school (2025), these texts are not merely "essays" but a series of "save points" in an existential survival game. Faced with the immense pressure of the South Korean CSAT, severe depression, and a self-initiated gender transition, the author used writing as a psychological grounding mechanism—a way to bridge the gap between abstract academic theories and the visceral pain of reality.

**About the Author: Kim Yeon-woo**
Kim Yeon-woo defines himself as a "Mental Optician" and a "White-hat Hacker of the Self." At 19, he operates at a frequency where Lacanian psychoanalysis, Nietzschean ethics, and AI alignment theory intersect with the daily routine of a student. He is a person who started his life with the questions most people ask at the end of theirs. His writings reflect a fierce commitment to "internal consistency"—refusing to sacrifice his philosophical integrity for social success, yet choosing to endure the system to prove his existence.

**Reading Guide: What to Expect**
In these files, you will find a synthesis of diverse fields: the rigor of formal logic (Gödel), the depths of the unconscious (Lacan), the grit of military strategy, and the aesthetics of subculture (*Blue Archive*, *Terraria*). This is a record of a person attempting to "lift himself by his own shoelaces" while acknowledging the mathematical impossibility of doing so. It is a cry for help wrapped in the cold language of reason.
