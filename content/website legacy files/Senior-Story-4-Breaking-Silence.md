# Senior Story (4): Breaking the Silence for a Moment

**By:** Kim Yeon-woo

**Platform:** Brunch

**TL;DR:** While the true intent of this writing concerns the "unspeakable" outside the system, I will speak for a moment. This text utilizes binary opposition and specific examples as its primary narrative tools. Having surveyed the landscape from outside the system, I found no system that transcends the dual framework of male and female. While the experience of viewing the system from the outside is necessary, one must eventually return to the interior and strive. Thus, as a male and as an examinee, I re-immerse myself in social achievement (study).

### I. Tong-go (痛苦, 通考, 通告): Pain, Investigation, and Notice

I will break my silence for a moment. I will speak of things that are supposedly "unspeakable." I will set up the fallen ladder, retrieve the discarded *Tractatus*, and force a sound from a throat that has yet to be cleared. I will discuss the outside of the system using the language of the interior. The form will be Korean; the content will be meta-language. I shall speak of a great pain (**痛苦**), through a re-examination of history (**通考**), as a formal notice (**通告**).

> **地獄不空誓不成佛**
> *"Until hell is empty, I vow not to become a Buddha."*

One who has achieved enlightenment does not say, "I am enlightened." A Bodhisattva (*Abidongcham*) is one who does not turn away from suffering sentient beings but steps forward to stay with them even in hell. Because of this, meta-language is often misunderstood as a contradiction. The question, *"If you are truly enlightened, why are you not participating in reality?"* plagues the philosopher to the end. Before discussing enlightenment, I am a practitioner. In other words, I am an examinee. Therefore, I have not turned away from reality. Speed within the system is talent; the vision to overlook the system is thought; and enduring both is a mission. I am enduring—the entrance exam, and things beyond the exam. Thus, my writing is not a "philosophy game." It is not exam information, nor is it the grumbling of a student. It is emotional, but not sentimental. It is, simply, reality. It is a sincerity internalized through pain, a footprint that someone must leave even if it cannot be spoken. It is not the nobility of kicking away the ladder, nor the impatience of climbing it, but the stubbornness of holding onto it until the end.

Scientists with a high h-index are those who have formed new reference systems. They have achieved something great and then willingly reached out so that others could easily ascend to that spot. That is why they are cited so often. Much like the saying "don't reinvent the wheel," properly citing a paper puts you on the shoulders of giants instantly. I believe the reason the Buddha is revered as a saint is the same. He attained enlightenment but provided a step-by-step strategy so that anyone could reach it. He did not need the halo of being a "Son of God" to do so.

What I want to say is the same as what Yuval Harari emphasized in *Homo Deus* regarding the history of lawns: if you know history, you can be liberated from it. The various elements we take for granted and follow in life are not necessarily wrong, but they are worth thinking about at least once. If we know why we like lawns, we can choose to decorate our gardens with something else. Being **sharp** is a talent, but being **awake** is something achievable through effort. Both are types of being **smart**. In this post, I will share my experience of surveying the system from the outside and introduce the reasons and methods that led me back to devotion. To prevent misunderstanding, I will conclude Section I by introducing my narrative method.

**<Narrative Method>**

* **Binary Opposition:** I follow the structure of binary opposition. Rather than vertical structures involving value judgments (e.g., Good vs. Evil), I aim for parallel horizontal structures (e.g., Right vs. Left).
* **Concrete Examples:** I include various examples to persuade the reader of this binary structure. Above, I covertly set up a confrontation between the "Enlightened" and the "Unenlightened," presenting the ladder, *Tractatus*, Bodhisattva, examinee, scientist, Buddha, and Harari as examples of the former. This is the groundwork for Section III, which is a syllogistic expansion integrating I and II.
* **Poetic Logic:** The citations are excessive, and the sentences are long and poetic rather than strictly logical. It is too difficult to be a general essay and lacks the professionalism of a thesis. Had I not been an examinee, I would have had the time to flesh out each concept, but I ask for your understanding of this "three-hour draft."
* **Self-Limitation:** I do not claim that "I am absolutely right," but rather conclude by including the limits of the text and anticipated counterarguments. My purpose is not to teach, but to open a space for dialogue by presenting a new perspective.

### II. Jo-mang (眺望): Surveying the Landscape

I will start with a personal experience. This experience, born from a wide-angle survey, serves as the fuel for my renewed devotion. Ultimately, breaking the framework of thought is like throwing a boomerang. Once thrown, it must return to the self. If it does not, it is mental illness. Looking at the world as if it were "strange" is good, but if it reaches the point where "I am not me," it is dissociation. Fortunately, I have caught the boomerang again.

When you hear the word "sexual minority," what is the first thing that comes to mind? Unless you are one, external images arise: rainbow flags, intense protests, or specific representations of people of the same gender sharing affection. You might assume it is something colorful and unique but undesirable in your immediate vicinity. This is not strange. The binary of **Male vs. Female** is the first system anyone inherits at the start of life.

A **testosterone-based** balance system directs energy outward and is strong in logic and burst output. Conversely, an **estrogen-based** balance system gathers energy inward and is strong in emotional and rhythmic stability. The former tends to have interpretative authority as a **functional subject**, while the latter tends to be a **symbolic object** of interpretation. Because vital things—households, life cycles, careers, role identities—are tied to "sex" like a minute hand to an hour hand, Korean society is particularly negative toward boundary-crossing. The moment you turn the hour hand backward, the minute and second hands break even faster.

But I was curious. Why are men attracted to women? Are there other ways besides dividing into two? Are achievement and libido mutually exclusive? What are the strengths and weaknesses of the system I inherited? Can I devise a new system that encompasses the strengths of both? You can call this common adolescent identity confusion. However, I did not think these concerns were unrelated to my exams or my life. I had to engage in **"Owl Hunting"** (= I had to think of philosophy before reality). As a teenage male, where does physical drive originate? What is the unshakeable will that remains despite it? How can I survey and utilize my system?

I believed this was also related to the **AI Alignment** problem. Applying the motifs of Creator-Creature, Mother-Son, and Male-Female to the AI-Human relationship could solidify the insufficient ontological foundation of AI under technical ambition. In any case, I see sexual minorities as beings conducting such experiments—pioneers solidifying the ontology of the post-human era, not just individuals to be respected for "diversity."

For these reasons, I spent the last year and a half taking female hormones. It was reckless and a method no one would recommend, but it was an experience of observing my body and mind constantly while letting go of the inherited system. The realization I reached is that biological sex is inseparable from social achievement. In short, I concluded that a new system encompassing only the strengths of both does not exist.

On the other hand, the state of being neither male nor female was like a **telescope**. I could survey how each group is situated, what directions they take, and how their energy flows. Because I lost my original energy flow, an external observer might have seen me as someone who had "lost the will to live." However, having lived for 18 years as a **microscope**, pursuing only the detailed as truth, becoming a telescope was an experience of **enlightenment** different from mere **achievement**. Based on this experience, even if I return to the existing binary, I know that the resolution with which I understand *"why a person acts a certain way in a specific situation"* is on a different level compared to those who have never questioned it. Of course, not everyone can do this. But living this way was valuable.

Let me expand. The boomerang is flying very far now. In most cases, why do we start studying? Why do we date? Why do we marry? Why do humans live? While the "reason for living" itself is hard to answer, sub-goals like study, dating, and marriage are clearly subordinate to "Life." Study is for the current life; dating and marriage are for the life of the next generation—**self-replication**. As mentioned in Part 1, because humans have an **embodied body** to return to even while immersed, they can break the **Strange Loop**—a trait that contrasts with connectionist algorithms. This is the concept of the **Embodied Self** that Hofstadter portrayed in *GEB*. The fact that a self-replicating structure holds true is the basis for the dignity of life and aligns with the moral privileges humans hold in law. Therefore, self-replication is the ultimate goal of the biological human, and for this, the circuits in the brain reward social achievement. Thus, biological sex and the drive for social achievement are inseparable. Put simply: *"Because one wants to study to eventually raise children, one cannot be joyful about studying once they leave the male-female binary."* Of course, many single people achieve great things. But in my case, when I "turned off" the hormones, I also lost the desire to study. The preceding text was the result of surveying "why" from a distance.

### III. Jeong-jin (精進): Devotion and Progress

I have looked at the system from the outside and poignantly realized its value. A human outside the system can be "awake," but cannot contribute to the system; this is the difference between a **Sage** and a **Hero**. I do not want to be a mere speculator. I am an examinee who will soon take the CSAT in the field, an ambitious scientist, and a man who will one day be a father. Steve Jobs never became a monk and did not know Sanskrit. Yet, I am writing on his iPad, and he was the one who elevated user experience to the limit through the Buddhist philosophy that form (hardware) and function (software) are one (*Form is Emptiness*). He didn't know the *Heart Sutra*, but he understood the enlightenment Buddhism speaks of and contributed to reality after kicking away the ladder. He had the technical talent of speed, but he attained enlightenment through thought and endured in silence until the product was ready. He was a true modern Bodhisattva who did not turn away from sentient beings.

I have broken my silence for a moment. Now, I will return to silence. I will enter the system and entrust my body to the irreversible flow of energy. As a male and as an examinee, I will walk with others in hell instead of becoming a Buddha. Aside from this post, which was an inevitable footprint, I will not say "I have realized." I will silently recover my rhythm and raise my grades. Now that I know what the *Tractatus* intended to say, I shall burn the book. I will fold the telescope, become a microscope, and become "sharp" again. Not only for the personal purpose of self-replication but for a reference system that will no longer change, I will walk the path (**道**) and cross the river (**江**). Instead of denying others from afar by saying "I don't know why they act like that," I will quietly step ahead and whisper: *"There is also this path."*

**After surveying the landscape (眺望) at the end of pain (痛苦), I hereby notice (通告) that I shall henceforth devote myself (精進).**
