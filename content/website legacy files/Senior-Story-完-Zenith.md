### I. War

#### I-1. The Iran-Israel Conflict

In June 2025, Iran and Israel engaged in 12 days of all-out warfare. Israel struck nuclear facilities and military leaders in night raids dubbed "Operation Rising Lion," while the US conducted joint strikes on specific sites. The highlight of "Operation Midnight Hammer" was not Israel’s domestic forces, but the US Air Force’s **B-2 Spirit** stealth bombers. Six of these aircraft executed a precise, multi-sequence mission: flying 18 hours from Whiteman AFB, Missouri, mid-air refueling, entering the Mediterranean, and penetrating Iranian airspace to drop 14 missiles that pierced 60 meters underground.

Knowledge, Scale, Endurance. These three pillars overlap perfectly with the educational goals of American military academies. A single B-2 flight path is the ideal of the West Point curriculum. Strategic bombing is not merely "dropping high-tech weapons"—it is the act of designing and maintaining a system to completion. War is a vast laboratory proving a nation's survival structure, and the academy is where the talent to operate that experiment is forged.

#### I-2. West Point (The US Military Academy)

West Point is a civilizational structure showing how America designs its survival. It is a factory for those who will survive, design, and command war. Education rests on three axes:

* **Knowledge:** Practical theories in science, math, diplomacy, and strategy.
* **Discipline:** Tactics and leadership.
* **Endurance:** Physical strength, mental fortitude, and tolerance for extreme pressure.

While Liberal Arts Colleges (LACs) produce "Thinkers," West Point produces "Judges and Stakeholders"—people who can dominate a battlefield at the moment of decision. Cadets are required to build structure amidst pain akin to death. A "West Point" background is a symbolic mark of leadership and judgment because it proves the individual survived a world of uncontrollable stress by exercising self-control.

#### I-3. The Korean High School is an American Military Academy

In South Korea, high school is not just the final stage of teenage education; it is a military academy for officer candidates preparing for a national operation: **The CSAT (Suneung).**

* **Knowledge:** GPA, CSAT, Essays.
* **Discipline:** Guidance, attendance, dress code, student records.
* **Endurance:** Concentrated study for 10+ hours, physical fitness tests, psychological management.

The results are quantified into a national ranking (Class Rank), determining "branch" (university/major) assignment. Teachers act as commanders, correcting deviations and ordering "supplementary training."

**Why this system?** It is rooted in **Imperial Japanese legacy** (Meiji-era total war education) and the **geopolitical reality of a suspended war.** In a country where immediate mobilization is a necessity, schools must produce "intellectual combatants" capable of high-speed information processing and extreme stress tolerance. The 38th parallel is unstable; thus, the education system is designed to sort the highest-caliber human resources for efficient distribution across the national front.

#### I-4. Creative West, Credentialed East

The US ecosystem has a dual nature:

* **West Coast (San Francisco/Silicon Valley):** A laboratory where dropping out is a badge of honor if linked to a startup. Portfolios (GitHub, papers) matter more than degrees. "Fail fast, Fail better."
* **East Coast (Boston/NY/DC):** An elite belt of traditional authority. Credentialing (JD, MD, PhD) is the barrier to entry for politics, law, and finance. GPA and recommendation letters are the fine filters.

#### I-5. Who Holds the Controls?

South Korea lacks the luxury of this dual structure. We don't have the resources for multiple success routes. Therefore, a single individual in Korea must embody **both West Coast creativity and East Coast discipline/credentialing.** I have decided to take the controls. I don't see Korean education as a submissive experience; it is a **treadmill** to fill my deficiencies. I am preparing West Coast creativity—the spirit of designing from scratch—separately, just as Admiral Yi Sun-sin prepared for war before the system told him to. At the same time, I accept the CSAT as the "Fighter Pilot Track." I am aiming for **Medical School** because it is the most precisely designed "branch" where mistakes are not tolerated and life-and-death judgment is required. I am not just going to university; I am boarding the fighter jet that is the Republic of Korea.

### II. This is my World

#### II-1. Terraria: A Simulation of Designed Life

*Terraria* is not just a game; it is a world where the player builds their own rules and order.

* **Survival:** Monsters at night, the need for shelter.
* **Growth:** Designing weapons, armor, and NPC housing.
* **Meaning:** The world becomes a biography of the player's choices and scars.

#### II-2. Hard Mode: The Rite of Passage

In *Terraria*, defeating the **"Wall of Flesh"** births a completely new world. This is the transition from high school to university/society. Old equipment (GPA/CSAT memorization) becomes "Basic Education," and new, more dangerous "monsters" (responsibility, uncertainty) appear. The exam period wasn't about making the final weapon; it was about getting the "First Shovel" to mine the real world.

#### II-3. Endgame Weapons

* **Zenith:** This weapon is a synthesis of every sword used in the journey (Night’s Edge, Terra Blade, etc.). It represents the state reached by one who honors every past choice and connects them. This blog is a material for my Zenith.
* **Terraprisma:** Obtained only by defeating the Empress of Light during the day with zero mistakes. It symbolizes the "Certified Elite"—perfection, focus, and total control.

#### II-4. Synthesis

This world is my world. It is the Republic of Korea, and it is the simulation of life I have built. I am pursuing both **Zenith** (the total sum of my memory and choices) and **Terraprisma** (the symbol of verified mastery). Even if this text becomes useless later, it is a necessary component of the "Me" that will soon be completed.

### III. Zenith

#### III-1. The Sequences

The **Zenith** is the condensation of "what kind of player you were." Similarly, **"The Sequences"** (LessWrong) is an experiment to create an integrated cognitive system of logic and reason. Rationality is not a single thought exercise; it is the accumulation of judgment, error, recovery, and learning. The Zenith belongs to the one who built their own framework for judgment, not just the one who got the most "right answers."

#### III-2. Rationality: Martial Arts of the Mind

Rationality is a practical martial art. It is the ability to reason coldly, the flexibility to say "Oops!" and change tactics upon failure, and the mental strength to accept the uncomfortable reality that "I could be wrong." To survive the modern world, one needs more than math; they need survival strategy, emotional control, and design capacity.

#### III-3. Journey Toward the Zenith

The Zenith is the blade that proves how I have lived. I have learned survival from the "Military Academy" of Korean education while embracing the creativity and design of the West. Handing both Zenith and Terraprisma is a declaration: *"I lived by my own rules, and that life has been verified."* > "Of course, I could be destroyed. But if I am to be destroyed, I will be the one to do it proactively. As long as I have hands and feet, I have the right to seek out my own destruction and walk toward it. I cannot run away. The moment I do, I will be devoured."

> — **Boris Jineman, *Children of the Rune: Winterer***

I will fight the battle of my life on the battlefield of my choosing.
