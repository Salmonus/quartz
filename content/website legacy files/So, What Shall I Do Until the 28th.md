### The Metaphor of the One-Eyed World

In a world where everyone possesses only a left eye, having both eyes is treated as a disability. Because you see the world through two lenses, each view is blurrier than for those who focus with only one. A world where everyone opens only their left eye and trains only that perspective defines "normalcy" as a defect and covers the "normal" person's right eye.

Only the left field of vision determines exam scores and social status. These one-eyed people fall into the arrogance of believing they are geniuses, unaware that they are the ones who are actually hindered. Amidst this absurdity, those who are truly "whole" must decide:

1. Will they sharpen their relatively blurry left eye through transcendent will to be recognized by the one-eyed, and then use their secret second field of vision to propose a new direction?
2. Or will they remain losers, left behind by an absurd world, forever comforting themselves with the hollow thought that they are the only "normal" ones?

I believe that my having two eyes is not meant to bind me, but to offer an additional direction to this world. No matter how blurry the connections may seem, if I strive constantly to perceive them, the outlines will appear, and the details will finally reveal themselves.

### Proof Through Action

The only power that allows one to overcome a situation is a firmer will. I am now a man of age, soon to be formally issued a Resident Registration Card. It is difficult, but being trained doesn't mean the pain is lessened. The pain of a bullet is enough to make both a trained soldier and a civilian scream.

I must prove through my actions that I am an individual with the courage to scale walls—that overwhelming diligence does not stem solely from blind, convergent thinking. Instead of blaming the Korean elite for their total ignorance of the importance of horizontal thinking, I should work harder to reach a position where I can actually inspire them.

---

### The Pearl Within

Honestly, the situation is desperate. There are many hardships. When these thoughts reach their peak, I feel like there is no one around me—no family, no friends. To be frank, it’s an excuse. But only *Arceus* is a Pokémon with perfect stats in every category.

If I seem slow right now, as if I have a disability, it is simply because I am doing what needs to be done. Perhaps the reason my surface feels rough to others is that I am harboring a useful pearl inside.

If the exams were postponed again, would I be able to cry like the others I’ve seen?
