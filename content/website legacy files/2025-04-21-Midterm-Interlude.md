### The Burden of Preparation

I am busy. Even maintaining a table of contents is a luxury. Just like every other paper-based exam I’ve experienced, the closer the test gets, the more I feel there is nothing left I can do. It is a desperate feeling when I think of the people I want to impress. However, when I imagine a future where I haven't gone through this process, the present feels better. This time, it’s not that I *couldn't* prepare; it’s that I *didn't*.

### Deteriorating Health

My health is steadily declining. Not only is the weight gain unsightly, but my knees hurt terribly. Every step feels like carrying a heavy burden; whenever I step into an elevator, I feel as if the entire cabin sinks because of me alone. The weight gain isn't proportional to muscle mass, so my body can't support itself, leading to back pain. When my back hurts, I can't sit and study, so I’m forced to stand up, which naturally puts additional strain on my knees and kills my concentration. I’m so heavy now that without the aid of a treadmill, it’s hard to run until I’m out of breath; even after just two minutes, I’m drenched in sweat and gasping as if I’d run for over ten. My running form looks waddly, and my leg muscles ache as if I’ve strapped on sandbags. Simply eating less would solve the weight issue, but my desire not to strain my body further through lack of stamina is stronger. I expect to continue gaining weight until November.

### The Vicious Cycle of Sleep and Study

Sleeping is agonizing. Physical illness is easy to self-diagnose, and easy for others to notice. It’s easy to take medicine, and you just need to rest to get better. Mental illness, however, cannot be self-diagnosed, and others can never detect it. To get medication, I would need a psychiatric diagnosis, but informing my parents of my current state would only escalate the problem. When I try to rest quietly, my thoughts spiral out of control, forcing me to grab onto something—anything. That "something" is study, creating a vicious cycle. Lack of sleep leads to a critical lack of stamina and makes mental management difficult. On days when I truly haven't slept, the mucous membranes throughout my body flare up, and I find myself constantly darting in and out of the bathroom.

### The Racehorse Mentality

An individual pushed to such a precipice should feel despair, yet as my body and spirit are destroyed, I feel a refreshing sense of focus on a single goal. While every other activity is restricted, I feel more immersed in my studies than at any other point in my life. Like a racehorse with its vision blocked and muscles tensed in a narrow frame for a single purpose—to sprint—my entire nervous system is adapting. I couldn't have failed to anticipate this situation. Ultimately, this is what choosing the "Jeongsi" (CSAT-track) means.

### On Conviction and the Food Chain

There are too many people in this world. I have decided to stop thinking about myself. Adding one to eight billion doesn't create change. It is better to be a stubborn individual who attempts something slightly different, even if it has nothing to do with "success."

I respect those who choose the smarter, more advantageous path for themselves. A common error of the weak is to console themselves when defeated by the strong, saying, "It's okay because I am Good and you are Evil." They don't realize that is the worst possible attitude. Yes, pursuing high ideals is good. Individuals with conviction and will are radiant and magnificent. However, such a person must also be prepared to stay silent when defeated by the stronger. Therefore, if you judge yourself to be the weaker, it is better to discard your convictions. Lower your posture, give up your stubbornness, and embed yourself within a crowd that will act as your shield. I deny the very term "survival strategy," but I find loud-mouthed weaklings pathetic.

The thing I am worst at is humility. You can tell just by how shamelessly I boast about these things that everyone knows but no one dares say. Yet, you will have to respect me. Because in the moment of defeat, I will sink calmly and quietly, as if I had won. Just as a cargo ship laden with gold and jewels remains silent as it is submerged, I will accept my limits in silence without wagging my tongue. I will not console myself. To be weak is a sin, and the loser is the evil. Perhaps I am talking like a young Batman charging alone into Maroni's hideout, knowing nothing of the real underworld. But isn't that precisely the secret to Batman’s popularity?

### Misanthropy or Humanism?

The warped mindset described above is what pushes people away from me—should I correct this? For the stability of the species to increase, diversity must be secured. If I am wrong, it is merely a loss of -1 from a population of 8 billion. From an engineering perspective, that is just a negligible decimal point. It would be a lie to say I don't desire success. It would be a lie to say I don't want to live an enviable life. But that is a result, not the aim. **Value is power; assets are acceleration.** I think there are too many people. So many that we might need to reduce the number a bit. I am not denying humanism. Nor have I fallen into extreme delusions like a certain person who hated the Jews. I am simply saying: let us live while actually thinking properly. And so, Kim Yeon-woo eats alone again today.

### The Art of the Entrance Exam

Participating in the South Korean entrance exam system is like learning a specific handwriting. it is about installing the entire suite of behaviors: writing fast, erasing fast, learning fast, and forgetting fast. Those skills have been forged significantly compared to last year. However, it seems a higher absolute level of that ability will be required, and I will continue to strive to discipline that very skill. I must secure high-level computational power and realize that the world is based on a somewhat deterministic model—that continuous change can be grasped through discrete data, and that even a seemingly complex new passage can be systematized and inferred. It looks monumental, but it isn't. In gaming terms, it's just the level of having taken down one boss early. No matter how daunting the CSAT is, I cannot avoid it forever. Let us keep attacking.
