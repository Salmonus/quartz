### On Perspectives and Differences

> "Hey, why the serious face? Loosen up a bit."
> "You're too skeptical. If you live like that, you won't even be able to be happy when good things happen."
> "Life was a winter to you, but I suppose it was a spring to me."
> "You’re different from me. Because our thoughts are different, there’s so much to say."

---

### On Solitude and Connection

> "You know, when you first arrived, it wasn't just that you were different. As I kept watching you, I couldn't believe someone could live such a 'boring' life. I felt a sense of mission—that I absolutely had to teach you something fun."
> "You were so different from me. Seeing you live in a world I didn't know always made me feel strange. A world I could never feel or experience, so to speak. When I was being stubborn, you were the only one who broke that stubbornness, even if you had to knock me out to do it. You... well, I felt like no matter how much I knew you, I could never know you fully. And that's why I could never get tired of you, right?"
> "Truth is, I wanted to hold onto you and tell you not to go. I know you have many things going on that I can't understand. But this time—I don't know why—I just had this feeling that it would be truly dangerous. Even knowing I couldn't stop you, I kept getting this feeling... this feeling that you wouldn't come back."

### On Fate and Miracles

> "Sometimes, I want to be rude. I want to stop being careful and just say whatever comes to mind. I wish I had someone I could be like that with, even for just a moment."
> "You trust yourself, like a true mortal. Their courage is beautiful, but incredibly fleeting."
> "Miracles don't happen. Miracles only happen to other people, in the past, in books, or in the future. They don't happen to me. We are all destined to live as we are until we die. The Demonic, the medium, the madman, the fool, and everyone else."

---

### On Survival and Will

> "As I thought, you are a voyager."
> "You aren't the type to give your heart easily to others. Perhaps you truly are the right 'medicine' for him."
> "Do not stake your 'will' on things that have no hope."
> "A murder committed within the heart is, in a way, more cruel than one committed against the living. There is no corpse, not even a shred of emotion remains, and reincarnation is a distant dream. Yet, it doesn't remain empty. What fills that void is... something like a scream, so loud it makes the heart go numb."

---

### On the Reality of the Struggle

> "I believe in real combat over practice, and survival over effort."
> "The teachings of the best master cannot compare to what a real enemy can teach, and the most hardworking student cannot be as desperate as one whose life is threatened."
> "At the very least, we can choose to die together. Let’s consider that an honor, Boris."
> "It's bitter. And from now on, it will be even more... bitter. More and more, everything..."

---

### A Brother's Last Words and the Strength to Live

> "See? It's something I can do, too. Not just Father... I, too, can kill someone. And so can you."
> "Someone who cherishes something becomes capable of doing anything. So you, too... cherish yourself. You are more precious than any treasure, more than *Winterer* or anything else. Just as I can do anything for you, you must be able to do anything for yourself. Protect yourself with all your might... so that you never die, never are abandoned... never hurt, and never wounded."
> "Your life is separate from mine. You follow only yourself. Do not listen to the circumstances of others. Never listen to the words of those who make you weak. After I am gone... you must possess a truly strong heart. No one will be your shield, and you can never let your guard down before anyone. It will be hard... but it is worth it. Because it is the work of surviving. It is the work of surviving until you have tested every single infinite possibility dwelling within your life."

---

### On the Density of Life

> "What you lack is will! You say a dead person's life is over, so why do you keep placing the value of your life upon their death? If you truly think it's over, then end it all and live a new life chasing your own desires—or! Shouldn't you live harder and longer, if only for their sake? As long as you cannot be immortal, you have no choice but to replace the lives they lost by increasing the density and value of your own life, if that is what you want!"
> "You haven't lived through the whole world yet, little one... Why are you trying so hard to endure? There is no one in this world who doesn't struggle. And yet, they live without hiding their desire to live, and their desire to live more excellently. Humans don't live just to die someday. We only live for the tomorrow that exists in every moment of being alive."

---

### The Final Resolve

> "We will fight, and if we lose, we will die. I will protect him with all my honor, and if he dies, I will avenge him. I cannot accept the sacrifice of others."
> "It is not over yet. There is plenty of time to fight for yourself. Life doesn't end that easily. Why can't you see the other side of the life left to you?"
> "If a devil could love, wouldn't the object of that love be only itself?"
> "At that time, I’ll be sitting on the stairs looking down. By my own power, I will start everything once again."
> "However, the fact that I have come this far is ultimately my own responsibility. I know that not everything goes as planned. I cannot stop now. I must go to the very end. Forgive me. No, do not forgive me."
> "Nothing changes, so you must keep going. Sing. Dance. Cry and laugh until the dawn comes."
