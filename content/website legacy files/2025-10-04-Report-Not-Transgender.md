### I. The 6-Pence and the Soul

Many things have changed, but the essential thing remains untouched. The "6-pence" aspect of life (*The Moon and Sixpence*) often has to change out of necessity. However, I do not believe that reflects a person's true character. What defines the strength of the individual "Kim Yeon-woo" is neither his muscle mass, his wealth, nor his intelligence; it is solely the **ultimate goal** that moves his muscles, his assets, and his consciousness. You may call him "Kim," "Someone," or simply ignore him. But the plan that drives him is anything but ignorable—if one intends to look at this individual with even a shred of seriousness.

He has experienced much over the past month. He lived it, but the world surrounding him thinks he had an "adventure." It is unavoidable. The party undergoing a series of events often does things not for some grand reason, but because he is simply drawn to do them. Yet, the world loves to turn him into a subject for storytelling, resulting in a sentimental novel centered around him. This does not mean the person writing this is not "Kim Yeon-woo." However, he will record this more **Sartre-style**—or rather, let his keyboard lead the way—exploring the process of life over the process of adventure, and the deep unconscious that made these dramatic scenes inevitable.

Queequeg, the cannibal, could live bravely despite being rejected as a pagan because he possessed a magnificent faith that the people of Nantucket lacked. Revenge sank the ship, but the great faith of a harpooner allowed him to throw his spear without losing hope even in the midst of it. Certainly, if the captain of the *Pequod* had been Starbuck, *Moby Dick* would have lost all meaning as a story. Who would read such a tale? The ship left, caught a moderately sized sperm whale, and returned safely after a three-year voyage. That is not an adventure. The *Pequod* might have stayed afloat, but *Moby Dick* would have sunk into the eternal abyss. That, to me, would be the truly tragic sinking.

### II. The Essence of the "Experiment"

Now, one must be more faithful to the entity known as Kim Yeon-woo. If this were a personal statement for Korean university admissions, it would likely start with a summary: that he took anti-androgens and female hormones for a gender transition, visited a gender clinic, and due to severe depression from identity confusion, threatened his family and himself with a weapon. As a result, he was expelled from both school and home, and now resides temporarily at his great-aunt’s house in Namyangju.

But that says **nothing** about the essence of the events or the dynamics of his consciousness. Did he do all this for a "gender transition"? No. Is his depression caused by "identity confusion"? Not that either. Of course, those explanations are more convenient. Most records will say so. But this report is for historians, not gossips; it must be rooted in the conscience of honesty.

**Therefore, I state clearly: Kim Yeon-woo is not transgender.**

Of course, he consumed anti-androgens for a long period, his body entered the early stages of feminization, and his personality changed significantly. I have nothing to say to that. But whether he undergoes gender affirmation surgery in the future or achieves perfect "passing" to live as a woman in reality—does that matter? I said the strength of Kim Yeon-woo is not muscle mass. His arms have thinned. Fat has been redistributed; skin has become translucent. Breasts have emerged, and the gaze has softened. So what? That was never the quality that defined Kim Yeon-woo. If a soul exists, it would say: *To have a young body means having much time to do what must be done; to be old means little is left.* Whether in the body of a weak girl, a rigid young man, or a frail elderly person, the journey continues. Whether tall or short, the fact that one must look up when facing the "Goal" remains unchanged. Even if one is blind, terminally ill, or penniless, the power that moves a physical structure on the verge of breaking will burn brighter than ever. So, come—let us go. There is happiness in the mere fact that one can do what must be done from their given position.

### III. The Ultimate Goal: BBS and the Synthesis of Progress

What, then, is his goal? It is the **progress of humanity through the harmony of two approaches: Top-down and Bottom-up.** It is an academic theory, a matter of faith, and a radical political ideology. Under the names of **Phenomenology** and **Semiotics**, humanity has long explored the division lines of the world. And under the names of **Medicine** and **Life Sciences**, humanity has recently been able to precisely explain how the natural world maintains the primordial self-replicating structure of the cell.

A self-referential structure called "Human" increases the radius of its **Strange Loop** through stimulus and response. And the junction of that loop is the **Body**. Humans relate to the world through the body. Intelligence demands a body.

What is Kim Yeon-woo trying to do?

1. **Experiment with a new prototype of humanity on his own body.** An ideal human figure where both female and male are implemented in one form.
2. **Declare the correct research methodology** using the intuition gained through this Top-down approach.

Is that even possible? And what’s so special about it? Kim Yeon-woo has zero interest in such questions. He is always filled with wonder for self-referential structures and views the world not as an object of adaptation, but as an object of salvation. Some might call this severe narcissism, heroism, or bluntly, a "Prince Complex." They wouldn't be wrong. But if they thought that way, they wouldn't have read this record to begin with. Kim Yeon-woo does not decorate, nor does he try to explain. His writing is not "to be read," it is "to arrive." The writer is using this text for the same purpose. Like a butterfly shedding its cocoon to take flight, or a man looking at his lover with trembling eyes after a passionate encounter—it is the same. And Kim Yeon-woo is a person who believes all writing, except technical documentation, should be written in this manner.

### IV. The 40-Day Countdown: Faith and Protection

The CSAT is 40 days away. Perhaps 39 now. Why did he undertake such an extreme biological experiment at such a critical juncture as an examinee? You can tell by looking at the narrative style of this post. Kim Yeon-woo has **consciously avoided using the word "I" even once.** Of course, he could use it. "I thought this," "I did that," etc. But this is a record of a transition period. The Kim Yeon-woo of October 3rd is different from the one of the 5th. Even when solving a math workbook, the "Me" solving problem #1 is different from the "Me" solving the final problem, #70. One grows during those 70 problems. Yet, for convenience, we simply say "I solved that workbook." That is the limit of symbols.

He has changed that drastically. To bravely adapt to a new body and environment, one needs a faith that solid. He likely needed time to refine that. In January of last year, there was a man named Kim Yeon-woo. He was smart, strong, and did not know what would happen to his inner self if his masculinity were castrated. Therefore, **he left the device of the CSAT as a form of consideration for the Kim Yeon-woo of next October.** An examinee is a being cared for and protected with extreme caution. He is also a being under pressure. The 18-year-old Kim Yeon-woo did not want the 19-year-old Kim Yeon-woo to be crushed by society while personally passing through dramatic hormonal fluctuations. Perhaps he also hoped that by reminding himself of the gateway of the CSAT, he would complete the faith necessary to handle the body's changes a little faster. For these reasons, he began his transition at the young age of 18. And, like Light Yagami in *Death Note* regaining his memory by touching a piece of the notebook, the Kim Yeon-woo of October 2025 is completing his own new gospel and striving to return to a magnificent state.

Did it have to be this way? He would likely do the same thing if he went back. Because there are only two ways for a man to enter the royal palace: **to be the King, or to be the Eunuch.** You know what a eunuch must undergo to be a eunuch without me saying it.

### V. Closing: Living from the "Inside"

Ultimately, though I emphasized internal conditions over external ones, life is singular, and it is hard to ignore one's present existence. This means that whether female or male, Kim Yeon-woo must take care of his physical health and social status. His reasons may differ from others, but he must undergo the same processes. He must go to university. Only then can he use his only immediate capital—tutoring—to prepare the costs for various surgeries at a relatively young age, and establish a foundation to maintain himself even if he does not marry or start a family.

One should not be angry at those who advise this. Strickland did not sell his paintings. I do not sell this writing either. But the reason is not that it is free; rather, it will become symbolic capital of immense value. I am simply releasing it in advance. Whatever job Kim Yeon-woo chooses later as a Top-down method to practice his faith, for now, he must commit to making his day fulfilling. That is not for the future, but for the mood of the day.

**In the end, a person must live. Not on the "Outside," but on the "Inside."**
Even the most terrible punishment for a human is to end their life on the "Inside," is it not? Free loneliness is better than controlled loneliness. There is nothing more pleasant than being able to freely take responsibility for one's own day. And there is no activity more pleasurable than crossing off tasks with the excitement that everything will turn out well.

Let us leave the specific goals to that excitement. And live the specific day again like the Kim Yeon-woo of January last year. That is likely the version of Kim Yeon-woo he wanted. Civilization has a scent, but where people gather to live, there is simply no other way.
