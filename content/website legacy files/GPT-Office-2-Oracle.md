# GPT’s Career Counseling Office (2): Auntie Oracle’s Cookie

**By:** Kim Yeon-woo

**Platform:** Brunch

**Context:** *The following is a reconstruction of a conversation held with chatGPT-5-mini on October 6, 2025, from 7:00 PM to 8:00 PM, regarding learned helplessness in transgender individuals.*

> **Spoiler Warning:** This text contains significant plot details from *The Matrix* film series.

---

### I. The Oracle and the Architect

[Video/Image: Neo meets the Oracle in *The Matrix* (1999)]

**Me:** In the movie *The Matrix*, what kind of entity is the Oracle?

**GPT:** The **Oracle** plays a vital philosophical and narrative role. Though called a "prophet," she is actually a program created within the Matrix system. Her primary function is to understand and predict human psychology, thereby stabilizing the humans within the system and maintaining balance. She doesn't just give "right answers"; she is a tester of choice and free will. As she says to Neo, *"You've already made the choice. You're just here to try to understand why you made it."* She represents the Delphic maxim, *"Know Thyself."*

**Me:** The Oracle appears as an ordinary grandmother you could see anywhere. On the other hand, the **Architect** appears in an overwhelmingly cold and authoritative manner. Why the contrast?

**GPT:** This is a key visual and symbolic choice. The Oracle’s appearance as a friendly grandmother suggests that truth comes from understanding and empathy. The Architect, in his perfectly refined suit and mechanical aura, represents the idea that logic alone can maintain a system without the need for friendliness.

---

### II. The Philosophy of the Cookie

**Me:** The Oracle knew exactly what Neo would say. She even predicted he would feel bad and handed him a cookie, saying he’d feel better after eating it. What does that mean?

> *"Here, take a cookie. I promise, by the time you're done eating it, you'll feel right as rain."*

**GPT:** She isn't just looking at the future; she is predicting human choice. Neo chooses whether to eat the cookie. The act of making that choice provides a sense of psychological stability. She leads him to experience a positive outcome through his own actions, thereby reinforcing his self-confidence. The scene conveys that **"Choice" and "Action" are the powers that change the human mind.**

**Me:** So it’s not that the cookie tasted good, but that he felt **Self-Control** (self-efficacy) by choosing to eat it.

**GPT:** Exactly. It is the act of choosing, not the cookie itself, that provides Neo with self-efficacy and stability.

---

### III. Learned Helplessness and Gender Transition

**Me:** Speaking of self-control, I heard at the psychiatric clinic of a gender clinic that transgender people often suffer from depression alongside gender dysphoria. One reason is that (unlike exercise or study) the physical changes from HRT (Hormone Replacement Therapy) or social approval happen externally, regardless of one's immediate effort.

If Neo’s avatar in the Matrix had been female while his real body was male, how would that have felt? In a situation where the self-image and reality don't align, what form would the Oracle's "cookie" take to drive Neo’s growth?

[Image Placeholder: A representation of self-perception vs. digital avatar / TSF (Trans-Sexual Fiction) motif]

**GPT:** Changes like HRT or social acceptance are often dependent on external factors. Psychologically, self-efficacy comes from the belief that "my actions can change the outcome." If too many changes are external, it's hard to feel control, leading to helplessness. If the Oracle were guiding someone in that situation, her "cookie" would likely be a device to restore **Self-Determination** through small, subjective choices. It would be a mechanism to make the person feel like a protagonist in their own transformation.

**Me:** So it’s an attempt to let Neo know the power of decision lies with him... but isn't that just a psychological deception? The Oracle can predict his mind, and the Architect can solve the Matrix like an equation. Isn't "choice" just an illusion?

**GPT:** Free will and predictability can coexist. In human psychology, the **process of making the choice** is what matters. Even if it's predicted, the moment Neo *feels* he chose, he experiences freedom. The Oracle’s "prediction" isn't a theft of the right to decide, but a tool to guide the experience of choosing.

---

### IV. Nutrients vs. The Dining Experience

**Me:** Imagine a 1000kcal nutritional capsule and a 1000kcal high-end meal. Even if the nutrients are identical, most people would choose the latter because the satisfaction of the *act* is more important than the *result* of proteins and fats entering the body. Isn't the Oracle’s cookie exactly like that? Whether you swallow a pill or enjoy a meal, you digest 1000kcal either way—but to the human, these are two entirely different lives. The Architect only cares about the 1000kcal result.

**GPT:** That is a perfect metaphor. For the Architect, "Result = 1000kcal consumed." Done. For the Oracle, the quality of the experience and the subjectivity of the choice are what matter. The cookie is a "Free Will Device" that includes the quality of human experience.

[Image Contrast: A man eating a luxury meal vs. a man taking a pill]

**Me:** But isn't the Oracle being cruel? She knows the Matrix has repeated many times and Neo's choice is part of the system. She knows the real "The One" is defined by the balance between Neo and Smith. Yet she still says, "Take a cookie, you'll feel better"?

**GPT:** It can look like psychological exploitation. But in Matrix philosophy, she isn't trying to change the result; she is a device to save the **Experience** within the process. Her "deception" is a way to give humans a sense of growth and agency.

---

### V. Becoming the Oracle of One's Own Transition

**Me:** Connecting this back to the transgender question: HRT results in feminization—that is a predetermined clinical result. But just waiting for the result can cause learned helplessness. From a trans person's perspective, a doctor is like the **Architect**: *"In a few months, this will happen."* One knows it rationally, but transition is more than that. Therefore, to have a satisfying transition, the individual must play the role of the **Oracle** for themselves.

**GPT:** Very interesting. The doctor provides the Architect-style information (the predictable, scientific facts). The Oracle role would be experiencing the change as something "I subjectively created." It turns waiting into an experience of active choice. To avoid helplessness, one must become their own Oracle.

**Me:** A doctor measures hormone levels, but people with the same levels aren't all the same. All males fall within a certain testosterone range, but they aren't the same *Man*. HRT puts a "Male" into the "Female" category, but what kind of **Woman** she becomes is something the trans person must design herself. That is the "cookie" part the Architect cannot provide.

**GPT:** Exactly. Identity is formed within choices and experiences, not just hormone levels or categories.

**Me:** For example, breast growth is a physical change. If a friend mentions, *"Hey, your chest looks bigger,"* the way one observes their own reaction and controls that response is how one learns what kind of woman they are. Or if someone accidentally bumps into your hips—the physical growth is a medical fact, but how you choose to react (shyly, confidently, humorously) is the opportunity to determine your womanhood. The physical change is just the beginning.

**GPT:** You’ve hit the core: identity is formed by external conditions + **the choice of how to act in those situations.** It’s about utilizing the change to experience subjective agency.

[Image: *Anne of Green Gables* - A symbol of coming-of-age and self-identification]

### VI. Conclusion: Installing the Drivers

**Me:** It’s actually a chance to "choose" one's personality once more. Transitioning provides new opportunities and choices one never had as a male. It's a continuous series of situations where you decide how to manage your new self. Becoming transgender is a declaration to embrace those choices. There is a sense of pride in that, as it's an opportunity most people never get.

**GPT:** So transition is not just a physical shift, but a process of designing and growing into the woman you will be.

**Me:** Even a "cisgender" man didn't necessarily choose to be "this kind of man" from the start. So rather than "choosing what woman to be," it’s more about **"experiencing what choices I make."** If you emphasize the predetermined result, you are an Architect (Despair). If you emphasize the joy of the process, you are an Oracle (Joy). Nietzsche said we only experience who we are. A trans person gets to experience who they are as a male in the first half of life, and who they are as a female in the second.

The fact that transition takes 3–4 years is actually a blessing. If a technology could instantly switch genders, the person would hit a wall. They would have new "hardware" without any "drivers." The long duration of transition allows one to meticulously test the **Driver** (social and psychological adjustment) to ensure the hardware functions properly in society.

[Image: *Overlord* (Light Novel) - A motif of adapting to a new, powerful body in a different world]

Accepting Auntie Oracle’s cookie is, in the end, about **taking responsibility for and enjoying the experience of life.**
