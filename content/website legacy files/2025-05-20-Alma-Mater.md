### I. The Alchemy of Honest Thought

Right now, I am observing the inside of my mind with great curiosity. The process by which creative motivation aligns itself into a tree for the sake of survival is likely different from any symbolism or even the latest **Attention-based translators**. The process of realizing that what I have sought to do my entire life is already within me—that, too, is likely a **Strange Loop**.

It is both surprising and a source of pride to me that an ordinary high school student, sitting in a library, speaking of the end of civilization in a language of vertical and emotional context like Korean, finds that his high-density reflections are the embodiment of transparent honesty. I produce only structures and questions so vivid they cannot be polished. I refuse to let anything else in, struggling only to speak the unspeakable. I am witnessing a puzzle piece carved not to be read by others, but simply to move forward—a byproduct of honesty.

The originality of thought, which has co-opted every genre and field in the name of survival, merely structures pain; it does not leave holes in logic. The will to drag this body forward and live is, in itself, the **supreme logic**.

### II. The Nourishing Mother (Alma Mater)

No matter how much one is a mere twig with nothing to its name, there is a clear desire to be part of the trunk. We grow up hearing the names of giants who guard the **nodes** (branching points) until we are sick of them. From the perspective of someone who has never climbed Everest, the focus is naturally on the **Summit**, not the slopes. Yet, growing up is merely an exchange value—realizing one is a "normal" person and securing the ability to manipulate symbols out of a desire for survival. A person’s chosen dream (**Figure**) does not represent who they are.

I prefer the term **"Alma Mater"** (Nourishing Mother) over "Undergraduate." The comfort that word provides is the motivation for this post. Affiliations may differ, and titles may vary. Someone might have seen the last sparks of Connectionism in Toronto; another might have sat before a nuclear reactor in Portland exploring the self-referentiality of consciousness at the atomic level. All must be respected under the dignity of academia (**Nullo Discrimine**).

Of course, the undergraduate institution may differ depending on when one first made up their mind. Yet, I believe that ultimately, everyone desperately wants to reach the ancient land of **Latium**. What that old territory symbolizes is not simple, but it likely concerns **PIETAS** and **VIRTVS**—meanings whose inflections (**declensions**) I revere—and the **Tao (道)**, the archetypal symbol of all thought. Those who have accepted that heavy spear do what they must, whenever and wherever they are. Why? Simply because it must be done.

### III. Meta-Writing and the Rule of Inference

Writing must be a training exercise in consciously choosing the "most inappropriate" answer. The word "Novel" itself means "new," and writing that contains the intimate structure of an individual—written under a name, not a profession—must naturally be fresh.

I have already deleted the previous sentence. Why? Because **[the reader]** likely already knows. I didn’t even include the word "the reader," I just put it in brackets. Because it’s obvious. The reason my writing describes itself meta-textually is that it *is* my question, my pain, and my structure. It is my belief that every human must secure this level of abstraction to choose their own focus. Because I remain so stubbornly silent, even if this text was produced in under an hour, you will have never seen a text like this before, even if it is poorly written.

There were things I was not born with.

> *"Read this passage and choose the most appropriate answer."* That ability is not the **Rule of Inference** of my particular language model. The fact that I choose to say "it is not the Rule of Inference of my model" instead of "I'm not good at solving problems," using engineering and math metaphors, reveals how vulnerable I am to the CSAT in general.

Yet, that ability is important. My conclusion is not "I am special." Rather, it is closer to the question, **"What am I capable of?"** Yes, I can choose the appropriate answer through training. And in **Nature**, there are answers. South Korean society judges it "too late" to realize this in the 9th grade.

Can someone who started so late still be protected under the shade of the **Alma Mater**? Can they be respected under the dignity of academia? Can they bet on becoming a **node**? The CSAT told me, "It is possible even if you start in high school." If not for the CSAT, I wouldn't have been able to set foot in the world of rules, however late. Thus, a Zen Buddhist attitude of stepping back just because one has grasped the essence is not allowed. To obtain a mother's mercy, I must first prove my gratitude. That gratitude is for allowing me to belong to the **Tree of Knowledge**. The moment I confirm that gratitude, the path to **Creative Mode**, not just **Survival**, will open. Honestly, there isn't much time left.

Existential thinkers are people who would rather jump and die right now if they cannot turn the manipulation of meaningless symbols into their own language. I wish society were kinder to such people.

### IV. Conclusion: The Sacred Puzzle

We do not grow a tree simply because it bears fruit. We provide sunlight and water because the tree itself is **sacred**. Another expression for sacred is **"fun."** It means it makes my heart race; it makes me feel excited and surging every time I sit at my desk. A puzzle is not enjoyed because of an intention; it is just enjoyed. Life is not saved because it is useful; it is just saved.

I feel at peace again. It means I have written well, that I have lived life well, and that I have brought a sip of a symbol system that I could never understand into my **TNT** for a reason other than survival. And so, slowly but surely, I must continue.
