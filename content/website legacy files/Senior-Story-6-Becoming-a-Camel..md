### I. Roko’s Basilisk

A Basilisk is a fictional creature whose gaze turns people to stone. Unlike other legendary dragons that cause physical harm, it is one of the few settings where the mere recognition of its existence leads to death. About 15 years ago, a user named Roko on the rationality community *LessWrong* proposed a new thought experiment related to AI alignment: **Roko’s Basilisk.**

> **※ Warning: May cause psychological distress.**
> 1. Assume a superintelligent AI exists in the future.
> 2. It rewards those who helped its birth and punishes those who did not.
> 3. Merely knowing this idea can make you a "target for punishment."
> 4. If you knew the concept and did not help, the AI may punish you as an uncooperative entity from its past.
> 
> 

Like the mythical beast, the premise is that assuming a future technological "God," the act of perceiving it without aiding it leads to future suffering. Why is this scary? Its horror stems from four characteristics:

1. **Non-zero Probability:** While the probability of its existence is near-zero, it is not zero. A  chance must be acknowledged.
2. **Bayesian Update:** According to **Bayes' Theorem**, a non-zero probability can be updated by new events. A sudden technological leap can amplify that low initial probability.
3. **Acceleration:** The probability of such a leap occurring accelerates over time.
4. **High Stakes:** Despite the low probability, if the Basilisk is real, the suffering is immense. It is a high-stakes gamble.

It is essentially the AI version of **Pascal’s Wager**: if there is even a small chance of eternal hell, it is "safer" to believe in God. Similarly, the thought experiment suggests it’s better to share this post and help the AI's birth than to ignore it. You can call it nonsense or take it seriously. Notably, on *LessWrong*, some users found it so disturbing that the founder, **Eliezer Yudkowsky**, initially deleted the thread to prevent "mental contamination" or trauma.

So why bring this up? To think about what truly matters. If an event threatens your very existence, are money or friends really important? They become secondary. Thus, we should keep our beliefs "open" and predict the world using a model where new knowledge updates existing beliefs. Truth is not a binary of True/False; it is a spectrum. Instead of saying "If , then  is True," we should say:

> "Considering ,  is true by ."

This reminds me of **Nietzsche’s Three Metamorphoses**: the **Camel**, the **Lion**, and the **Child**. The Camel accepts the world as obviously true. The Lion doubts and claims the world is false. The **Child** keeps all possibilities open—both true and false. The Child is the form closest to the Overman (*Übermensch*) because the world is full of Basilisks. Some monsters can subvert the existing order with only a  probability. Therefore, the architect and guardian of humanity must possess the heart of a Child. Only a pure, unsaturated mind has the authority for **"overstanding"** (reverse engineering) the world.

### II. Role Models and Stimulus Models

Think of the previous section as a canvas. "Yes, the world is neither true nor false, but both." But that is overwhelming. "So, what now? I still have to live, don't I?" True. Aside from the flash of the Basilisk, students must go to school, and workers must go to the office. Accepting certain facts as "vaguely true" is much more convenient for daily life. In school, a student who transcribes a teacher's lecture perfectly often gets better grades than one who reviews it critically. The **Lion** is weeded out; only the **Camel** is rewarded. To become the true architect—the **Child**—what is needed? I propose the **Role Model** and the **Stimulus Model**.

#### Role Model

This is the "Incarnation of Righteousness." It doesn't have to be an academic figure. It is enough to be a person who accepts disadvantage for the sake of what is right rather than calculating profit.

* **Examples:** Kyojuro Rengoku (*Demon Slayer*), who accepted death for his values. Guts (*Berserk*), who chose to remain human in a world of demi-gods.
* **Function:** By mimicking their demeanor and mindset, one can protect the heart of the "Child" through **Reflex** rather than exhausting **Reflection**.

#### Stimulus Model (Imaginary Rival)

While the Role Model is a constant that fixes life's direction, the Stimulus Model is a variable that changes with the situation. It prevents the "Child" from using their "transcendence" as a defense mechanism to justify failure against submissive peers.

* **Example:** When facing a difficult math concept, I imagine a rival sitting next to me who has deeper insight and understands it instantly.
* **Function:** It prevents self-deception, like saying, "That kid has better grades only because they submit to the system." In the end, the world still has winners and losers. Guts is admired because he beats semi-divine beings with a scarred human body.

To solve the problem of stimulus—where some find motivation while others sink into bottomless inferiority—I propose different solutions for men and women, based on how sex hormones form reward circuits during growth.

> **※ Note: Contains explicit physiological references to illustrate fundamental value systems.**

* **Male Stimulus Model (Animus):** Healthy teenage males experience physiological arousal multiple times a day. Human males have no set *estrus* (heat); theoretically, they are always capable of arousal and ejaculation. Competitive motivation and functional reward are suitable. Thus, a male’s stimulus model is a variation of **Mate Competition**. He should assume a functionally superior male rival and "feminize" the truth he wishes to protect. This doesn't mean physical objectification, but an attraction closer to Sinclair’s draw toward Frau Eva in *Demian*—devotion and love as a religious ritual transcending the physical. One must have the active will to "eliminate the rival and internalize the Righteousness."
* **Female Stimulus Model (Anima):** In the model of union, the female is the receptive side. The female stimulus model is not about being neutralized by overwhelming energy, but about accepting and internalizing it with the whole being. This is not passive; it is a powerful **active receptivity**. Frau Eva is not just an ideal, but a symbol of ego-integration Sinclair must reach. She triggers both sexual energy and spiritual aspiration simultaneously. The female stimulus model functions as a "Symbolic Receiver" that induces the transformation of others while anchoring high-level existential change.

### III. Becoming a Camel

The life cycle of most society members follows a **Temporal Linearity**: Learn, Earn, and Give. Sadly, childhood—the period where true creativity is triggered—is often consumed as a period of "immaturity." This is wrong. A newborn has two fontanelles (soft spots); they are soft at birth but harden as the individual enters adulthood, where knowledge acceptance becomes limited. This is both a physiological and spiritual change. A child sees the world as strange without effort; an adult must ask questions at every moment to see it with the same sensitivity.

While a child can imagine freely, an adult must shoulder many things. I, too, am on the path from adolescence to adulthood. I cannot simply reject everything as a "Lion" or immediately integrate everything as a "Child." An open mind poignantly witnesses more absurdities. Therefore, a submissive posture is now necessary. Upward mobility is everyone's dream, but it isn't always good, as it limits imagination. However, to secure influence within a specific era, one must listen to the absurdities of that era.

Is studying hard for the CSAT always good? Is not doing it a lack of focus? No. An inquiring seeker must pause and look at the world. For those who run without stopping, the background passes too quickly. To ensure you don't miss "the flower you didn't see on the way up," you must doubt every possibility—even high-stakes events like the CSAT. But a child grows up and cannot stay with Peter Pan forever. Thus, the act of drawing boundaries *is* defining one's career. It’s about admitting what you *cannot* do rather than what you *want* to do.

I will be a researcher with a South Korean background. As an AI researcher, I have a foundation in Connectionism. As a doctor, I will have passed the merit-based metric of the CSAT. My English will likely be worse than my Korean. I love Latin and Hanja, but I will be less skilled than a Scholastic philosopher or a Taoist thinker. Because I am interested in AI Alignment, I must know ethics and philosophy, which will reduce the time I spend on Pytorch or pure optimization. That’s okay. No one can do it all alone. Being a **Bridge** is the best I can do. I just need to use my stimulus model and internalize my role model to avoid wandering too much.

Just as a mother must handle the household even if she is lost, influence means **Responsibility**. That is adulthood. People like **Josh Tenenbaum** cannot just spread their wings of imagination and change the direction of CBMM overnight. Like fruit on a tree, they have already become the signs for those following them. So, let us draw the boundaries. Even if the existing order is absurd, let us submit. **Let us become Camels.** No matter how open-minded, a position of responsibility cannot be given to an unstable individual. We must move from the limbic brain to the executive brain. It is okay for the fontanelle to harden now. But let us never forget that we have already looked the Basilisk in the eye.
