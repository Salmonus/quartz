### 🐟 The Origin of `Salmonus_Kim`

Wanna hear the origin story of the nickname **'Salmonus_Kim'**? It’s a bit of a classic. 

My Korean name is **Yeonwoo Kim**, and *'Yeonwoo'* sounds suspiciously like the Korean word for salmon (*yeoneo*). Naturally, my friends started calling me **Salmon**. 

> [!abstract] The "Latin" Twist
> Back in elementary school, I had this weird obsession with Latin. I didn't actually know any Latin, of course, but I desperately wanted to sound like a scholar—or at least master the art of **Dog Latin**. So, I just blindly slapped the masculine suffix **'-us'** onto it.
